# Scroll Reference Notes

The reel is partially obscured by Instagram’s playback and account UI, but the visible reference establishes a clear direction: a dark, immersive web scene is revealed as a short cinematic sequence rather than a conventional static landing page. The visual treatment favors a large environmental backdrop, depth layers, restrained text, and paced transitions that let the scene carry attention.

For Lexora, this will become a scroll-driven legal world rather than a direct copy. A fixed Earth-like legal globe will move slowly behind the content while the page changes chapter mood as the user travels: **orbit / global context**, **research / network**, **evidence / archive**, and **resolution / clear path**. Text and small legal interface artifacts will enter in short offset sequences, while the world-scale background keeps the sections feeling connected.

The reference will be adapted through original composition, original generated assets, and Lexora’s established Midnight Docket and Verdict Green brand system. The implementation will avoid autoplay video, preserve normal browsing behavior, and provide a stable reduced-motion fallback.
